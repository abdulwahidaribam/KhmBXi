# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

KhmBXi aims to be a welcoming place for people to discuss and contribute to the project, regardless of experience, identity, background, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and code; do not attack people.
- Accept feedback and differing technical opinions in good faith.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing another person's information without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized conduct without consent, doxxing, and repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable in project spaces. This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces used for KhmBXi work.

## Enforcement and reporting

The maintainer is responsible for responding to reports and may edit or remove content, issue a warning, or restrict participation temporarily or permanently depending on the circumstances.

For a private concern, contact the maintainer using a private contact method listed on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/abdulwahidaribam). Do not use a public issue for sensitive reports. Reports will be handled as privately as practical.
